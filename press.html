Long

Srinivasan (Srini) Venkatramanan is a Research Assistant Professor at the Biocomplexity Institute, University of Virginia, where he works on computational modeling and simulation for biological and socio-technical systems. His research spans network science, stochastic processes, and agentic AI systems, applied to epidemiology, human mobility, healthy buildings, and neuroscience. He has contributed to national collaborative efforts including CDC FluSight, the COVID-19 Forecast Hub, and the Scenario Modeling Hubs, and his work has been funded by multiple grants from CDC, NSF, NIH, and ARPA-H. In 2022 he received the University of Virginia's Collaborative Excellence in Public Service Award for modeling support provided to the Virginia Department of Health throughout its COVID-19 response. He holds a Ph.D. in Electrical Communication Engineering from the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.
